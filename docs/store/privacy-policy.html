Human Tools Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · Applies to the Human Tools extension for Google Chrome

In short: Human Tools has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer receives none of your data. Text from a page leaves your device only when you press a button that asks for it, and it goes only to the service that button uses, with an API key you supplied yourself.

What stays on your device

These are kept in Chrome's storage for the extension, on your computer only. The developer never receives them.

  • Your API keys and chosen AI provider. These are the keys you enter in Settings for OpenAI or OpenRouter, and optionally GPTZero, Brave Search and Browserbase. They stay until you clear them in Settings or uninstall the extension. Chrome stores them unencrypted in your browser profile, as it does all extension storage, so anyone with access to your profile could read them.
  • The last panel you opened, so the side panel reopens where you left it.
  • Inspector results for your open tabs, so they survive closing and reopening the panel. They are held in session storage and are deleted when you quit Chrome.
  • Recent AI replies, kept in memory so repeating a request doesn't spend your key twice. They are discarded whenever Chrome stops the extension's background process, which happens often.

Results are kept per tab and discarded when that tab navigates or closes.

Several features run entirely on your device and send nothing anywhere: the reading-grade score, the Security panel's page check, and the Application panel's page check. The Application panel shows the names of a page's cookies, never their values, and only counts the page's stored items.

The domain-age lookup, link following and direct source fetches send no cookies and no referring page. Requests to the AI, GPTZero, Brave Search and Browserbase services carry your own API key for that service.

Third-party services

OpenAI, OpenRouter, GPTZero, Brave Search, Browserbase and rdap.org are independent services. Each one handles what it receives under its own privacy policy and terms. For the paid services, your use falls under the account you created with them. Read their policies before adding a key. The developer has no access to what you send them.

Changes to the pages you visit

A rewrite changes only how the page looks in your own browser. Nothing is sent to the website. Restore original puts the page back, and reloading the page undoes every rewrite. The highlights and numbered badges the extension adds are also local to your browser.

What the developer does not do

  • Collect, store or receive any of your data, on any server.
  • Sell or transfer your data to anyone, or use it for advertising.
  • Use your data for anything unrelated to the feature you asked for.
  • Use your data to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
  • Track your browsing. The extension runs nothing on a page until you open its panel.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Features that send data only run when you press their button, so not pressing it means nothing is sent.
  • Leave the optional keys empty and GPTZero, Brave Search and Browserbase are never contacted.
  • Clear any key in Settings and press Save to delete it.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored on your device.

Children

Human Tools is not directed at children under 13. The developer collects no data from anyone, children included.
